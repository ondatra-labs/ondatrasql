# Code of conduct

OndatraSQL follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repository, its issues and Discussions, and every other OndatraSQL community space.

In short: be kind and specific, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and unrelated promotion are not accepted.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through [ondatra.sh/contact](https://ondatra.sh/contact/). Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove content, or block someone from the project, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
